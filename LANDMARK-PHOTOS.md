# Location hero photographs

Downloaded from Wikimedia Commons on September 23, 2026. These are real location photographs, not Resolve job photos or claims of affiliation. Historic downtown views are used for Manheim and Denver; no claim is made that any selection is objectively the most famous landmark.

Assets are hosted locally in `src/assets/landmarks`. Astro generates responsive WebP derivatives; CSS crops them for display. Image derivatives retain the linked source license, including ShareAlike where applicable. Credits and license links appear with every photograph. No other edits were made.
